PCamVideo Support

Effective date: October 5, 2026

PCamVideo records video on an iPhone. A computer is optional. Finished files stay on the iPhone and, only if you connect one, on a computer on the same local network. They are not uploaded to the developer’s servers.

Use it on the iPhone

Open the app and tap Record on this iPhone. Choose 720p, 1080p, or 4K, then record. The saved file includes the microphone. The live preview is silent and lower resolution.

Three-second clips are optional. The app counts down for 2 seconds, records for 3 seconds, and joins those clips into one file when you tap Finish take.

Optional computer

If a Mac or Windows computer running PCam is on the same Wi-Fi, the iPhone can find it and send finished files to a folder you choose on that computer. You can record without a computer. Files recorded on the phone are sent after a later connection succeeds.

Privacy

The app uses the camera, the microphone, and the local network for recording and for an optional transfer to your computer. It does not require an account. It does not sell data and does not show ads.

Uninstalling the app removes recordings stored by the app on that iPhone. Files already copied to your computer stay on that computer until you delete them.
